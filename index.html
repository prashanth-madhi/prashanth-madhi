about

Hola Amigos, I’m a Senior Delivery Leader at Scotiabank with 15+ years of experience leading engineering and architecture teams in cloud migrations, scalable platforms, and high-availability systems across banking, insurance, and telecom.

I was born in Coimbatore, South India to a Telugu family, grew up in Queensland, Australia, and Guelph, Canada (Centennial CVI). I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo and remain a proud alumnus and mentor. A world traveler and multipassionate creator, I explore technology, filmmaking, culture, and community impact.

I’m an Indo-Canadian filmmaker, actor, and content creator. I’ve hosted live TV Show, Canadian Mosaic (AWAZ), acted and did casting direction in the short film Back to Normal, and frequently mentor and speak at universities.

I would like to empower education with Professional Masterclass Academy (PMA), create memorable journeys with Travellite Pro, and give back through SickKids and Canadian Blood Services (see my story).

Thanks for dropping in — let's connect wherever you prefer!
